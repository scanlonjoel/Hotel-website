Raising comfort to the highest level

Welcome to Luviana Hotel Resort

Luviana is a small coastal resort for people who want the trip to feel unhurried. Rooms open onto gardens, the kitchen cooks with whatever came in that morning, and the beach is a short walk with nowhere you have to be.

Stay for a long weekend or a slow season. Either way, we keep the noise down and the linens cold.
